# Battle City
---

## What is Battle City?

Battle City is a free, online multiplayer game where you drive a tank around a huge shared map. You can found a city and run it as its mayor, or apply for a job in a city someone else built. Everyone plays at the same time, in real time.

Your goals are to keep your city safe and to take on the cities of other players:

* **Build.** Mayors place houses, factories, turrets, hospitals and research labs to grow their city and its income.
* **Defend.** Citizens patrol in their tanks, repair damage and fight off raiders.
* **Attack.** Teams raid enemy cities, and a city can be destroyed when its enemies get through its defences.
* **Team up.** Chat with your city, with everyone, or whisper to one person. Cities rise and fall on how well their players work together.

Battle City was first released in the 2000s. It is open source (GPLv3), so the game belongs to its community and will stay free.

## Where is the project going?

This version of the game, "Battle City NG", is about making Battle City easy to play anywhere again:

* **Play in your browser**, with nothing to install.
* **Play on Windows or Linux** with a downloadable game.
* **Run your own server** on Windows or Linux.
* **Fewer crashes and bugs**, and smoother movement of other players' tanks.
* **Automatic builds and releases**, so fixes and new versions reach players quickly.

The steps, and how far along each is, are on the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
